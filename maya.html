01What Maya does

Most people don't need a diagnosis.
They need to understand what they're looking at.

A result you can't read. A symptom you can't place. A leaflet written for someone else. Maya sits in that gap — turning medical information into something you can actually act on, and telling you plainly when the answer is a clinician.

01

Reads your symptoms in context

Duration, pattern, what changes it, what came before. Maya asks the questions a clinician would ask first, and holds the answers together.

02

Explains the medicine, not the jargon

Findings, ranges and terminology rewritten in plain language — with the reasoning left visible, so you can see how the explanation was reached.

03

Knows when to stop and escalate

Red-flag patterns are matched before anything else is said. Below its confidence threshold Maya hands you to a human — with the conversation attached.

04

Shows its sources, every time

Every claim traces to published clinical guidance. Nothing is asserted from the model's own memory, and unsourced statements never reach you.

02How Maya understands

Six steps between your question
and a sentence you can trust.

Maya does not answer from memory. Every response is assembled — structured, retrieved, reasoned, then checked against the guidance it came from before a word is shown.

01

Listen

Free text becomes structured clinical concepts — onset, duration, severity, context.

02

Screen

Red-flag patterns are matched first, before any explanation is attempted.

03

Retrieve

Published guidance is searched semantically, then reranked for clinical relevance.

04

Reason

Retrieved evidence is weighed against the structured picture, never against memory.

05

Check

Each claim is verified against its source. Unsupported statements are dropped.

06

Explain

The answer is written in plain language, with the reasoning and sources kept visible.

06Trust & safety

What Maya will never do.

In medicine the constraints are the product. A model that will say anything is not a medical assistant — it is a liability with a chat window. These are enforced outside the model, where the model cannot argue with them.

Diagnose you

Maya explains what findings mean and what they are associated with. Naming your condition is a clinical act, and it stays with a clinician.

Recommend or adjust a medication

No doses, no substitutions, no stopping. Maya will explain what a drug is for and what to ask about it — nothing that changes what you take.

State anything it cannot source

Each claim is checked against the passage it came from. If the passage does not support it, the sentence is removed before you see it.

Talk past a red flag

Warning patterns are matched before the explanation is written. When one appears, the answer becomes a referral — and it does not soften it.

Guess when it is unsure

Below its confidence threshold Maya says so plainly and escalates to a human, with the full conversation attached so nothing is retold.
