02What Maya does

The manual has the answer.
Nobody has time to find it.

A fault at the far end of a site. A part number that has been superseded twice. A service interval buried in a 900-page PDF. Maya is the layer between the person holding the spanner and everything the manufacturer already wrote down.

01

Diagnoses by symptom

Describe what the machine is doing. Maya ranks the likely systems by confidence and tells you what to check first — in field order, not manual order.

02

Finds the exact part

Model, serial, build variant, supersessions and aftermarket equivalents — resolved against the catalogue, never generated from memory.

03

Tracks service by hours

Intervals against real meter readings across the fleet, so the next job is a date and a part list rather than a reminder to check.

04

Stops at the safety line

Pressure testing, lifting, stored energy. Where a procedure is dangerous, Maya says so and hands the job to a qualified technician.

03Equipment visualisation

Every system, on the machine itself.

Not a diagram of a generic excavator — the actual assembly, with each system isolated where it physically sits. Select a system to bring it forward.

04AI troubleshooting

Describe the fault. In plain words.

Maya structures what you said, screens it for safety, retrieves the relevant service procedures and ranks the likely causes — then tells you what to check first.
