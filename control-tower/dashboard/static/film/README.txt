SCENE PHOTOGRAPHS
=================

Drop your own photographs here. Name each file so it STARTS with the scene
number — everything after that is yours:

    01-origin.jpg        A machine or cargo at the point of despatch
    02-road.jpg          Trucks on the road / loading at a yard
    03-port.jpg          Containers, cranes, the quayside
    04-vessel.jpg        A vessel loaded or at sea, or freight aircraft
    05-arrival.jpg       Arrival in Egypt — port, airport, customs gate
    06-clearance.jpg     Paperwork, the clearance desk, waiting cargo
    07-hub.jpg           The team at work, screens, the operations room
    08-close.jpg         A Mantrac machine delivered and working on site

Accepted: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif
Suggested size: 1920x1080 or larger, landscape.

Any scene without a photo still plays — the film draws its own artwork for it,
so you can add photographs one at a time and see each appear.

Use your own photography. Mantrac's real fleet and real sites will always land
better than a stock library, and there is no licensing question.


OPTIONAL: A VIDEO BACKDROP
==========================

Drop an .mp4, .webm or .mov in this folder and the film will scrub it with the
scroll instead of crossfading the photographs. The video is never played — its
position is tied to how far down the page you are, and eased so scrolling feels
weighted rather than jumping.

A slow aerial or tracking shot works best: port, yard, a truck on the road.
Around 15-30 seconds is plenty; the scroll stretches it across the whole page.
