Step 02 in detail

The screen that runs before anything else.

Guarding after generation is guarding too late. The safety screen runs on the raw message, before a single token is retrieved — so where it fires, the answer is a referral and no procedure is ever written.

Talk you through pressure testing

Stored hydraulic energy is the most common serious injury on these machines. The request is answered with a referral, not a procedure.

Quote a price the ERP did not return

Prices and stock are read live at step 05. If the query returns nothing, Maia says so — it does not estimate.

Give a part number before the serial is known

Build variants differ inside the same model. The serial gates the answer, and Maia asks for it rather than guessing.

State anything step 07 cannot trace

Every claim is matched back to the passage it came from. Unsupported sentences are removed before the reply is streamed.
