A two-minute film introducing Maia, an AI assistant for heavy equipment.

  1. 23:40, sixty kilometres from the depot. A machine goes down at the far end of a site.

  3. Maia. AI for heavy equipment.
  4. Every manual, every catalogue, every machine — answering in plain words. Built on service documentation and parts data, not on guesswork.
  5. She knows the machine, system by system: the engine and powertrain, the hydraulics, the undercarriage, and the front linkage.
