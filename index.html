Maia — a film by Mantrac

A two-minute film introducing Maia, the AI assistant built for Cat® equipment customers at Mantrac Egypt.

  1. Intelligence that redefines how machines get supported. Built where data, machines and people meet.
  2. Equipment doesn't wait, and neither should support. A fault code at 11pm, a machine down, a project stopped, the call centre closed. That era is over.
  3. Maia. The AI assistant built exclusively for Cat® equipment customers. Always on, always ready, always yours.
  4. Your colleague at Mantrac who knows every part number, every fault code and every machine. She turns the noise of downtime into a single point of action.
  5. The goal: 94 percent faster first response. Parts lookups, fault diagnoses and service tickets answered in seconds instead of shifts. This is a pilot target measured against Mantrac's own baseline.
  6. She knows them all: the CAT 336 excavator, the CAT D6 dozer, the CAT 950 loader and the CAT DE550 generator set.
  7. From fault code to fixed. A customer reports fault E232 on a CAT 336. Maia identifies a restricted hydraulic filter, recommends part 462-1171 at 1,850 Egyptian pounds, in stock in Cairo, then reserves it and books a technician on ticket SRV-826041 with a four-hour response.
  8. Behind the scenes: telematics, the parts catalogue and service history feed Snowflake Cortex Search over 2.4 million part vectors. Retrieval and guardrails produce a grounded, cited answer in about 1.8 seconds.
  9. The hard part is finding one part among millions. Vector search narrows 2,410,000 candidates to 128, then to a single exact match at 0.94 cosine similarity.
  10. What she will never do: invent a part number, quote a price the ERP did not return, recommend before verifying the serial, or guess when she is not sure. Below the confidence threshold she escalates to a human with the transcript.
  11. Latency budget: 40 milliseconds to embed the query, 90 for vector search, 60 to rerank, 110 for the ERP lookup and 400 to first token — 700 milliseconds to the first word on screen. This is a target budget to be validated in pilot.
  12. Everything in one chat: diagnose faults, order parts, and route tickets.
  13. Everything revolves around your uptime: diagnose any fault code, find and buy parts, route your tickets, and speak your language — English and Egyptian Arabic.
  14. Say hello to Maia. Available now at Mantrac Egypt.
